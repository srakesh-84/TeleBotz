Use cases

Another way for people to reach your place.

TeleBotz creates a virtual entrance for physical spaces so visitors can connect with staff before they travel, arrive, or walk in.

Example pilot setting

Schools & colleges

Parents, students, and guardians can speak with admissions or reception staff without needing to travel first. It helps answer questions faster and improves accessibility for remote families.

Clinics & hospitals

Patients and families can check in, ask general questions, and connect with front-desk or care coordinators before arriving onsite.

Hotels & hospitality

Prospective guests can connect with on-site teams to ask about rooms, amenities, check-in timing, or special requests before they book or travel.

Showrooms & offices

Customers can approach a real team member to ask about products, services, appointments, or office access without waiting in person.

Businesses with visitor flows

Front desks, service centers, and facilities can create a clearer visitor entry experience while staff remain focused on the people already in the building.

Public-facing spaces

Organizations that receive repeated visitor questions can provide a simpler, always-available connection for first contact.
