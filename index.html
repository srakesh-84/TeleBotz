The TeleBotz platform

Two mobile apps. Optional robot hardware.

The smartphone is the core of TeleBotz. The User App connects to the Robot App running on a phone at the remote location.

User App

Installed on the remote user's smartphone to start and control a telepresence session.

Robot App

Runs on the smartphone placed at the destination, providing the remote camera, display and communication interface.

Optional hardware extensions

Motorized hardware

Add pan-tilt movement or other motorized positioning when remote physical orientation is needed.

Non-motorized hardware

Use a fixed stand, mount or tabletop arrangement to position the smartphone at the destination.

The idea

Not another video call.
A virtual presence at your premises.

TeleBotz is a smartphone-based telepresence platform built around two mobile applications: the User App and the Robot App. The apps provide the connection; optional hardware can add movement or a dedicated phone mount.

Your physical premises

Your receptionist or staff remain at the location and continue attending to in-person visitors.

Your virtual entrance

A remote visitor connects online, observes the reception area through the camera, and interacts with your on-site team.

Simple visitor journey

From a remote visit to a real conversation.

TeleBotz is designed to make the experience feel closer to approaching a person at a reception desk—not joining a scheduled online meeting.

01

Visitor enters virtually

The visitor opens the TeleBotz User App on their smartphone and connects to the destination phone running the Robot App.

02

Looks around

The visitor sees the remote location through the Robot App. With optional motorized hardware, the camera can also be moved remotely.

03

Connects with a person

The on-site receptionist attends the remote visitor while continuing to serve people who are physically present.

Designed for visitor engagement

A reception experience that is ready for visitors.

The experience combines connection management with a sense of physical presence.

Automatic answering

Visitors can enter the virtual reception without waiting for someone to manually accept a conventional call.

Automatic reconnection

If the network drops unexpectedly, TeleBotz can attempt to restore the interrupted session.

Callback support

When a conversation is disconnected, the system can attempt to reconnect participants, subject to the configured callback rules.

Remote pan-tilt view

Visitors can orient the camera to get a simple view of the reception space, rather than seeing only a fixed camera angle.

Visitor waiting state

If staff are busy, the visitor can be shown that the reception is occupied instead of being left uncertain.

Business-controlled hours

The business decides when its virtual entrance is available and can make it unavailable outside operating hours.

Where it could help

Give people another way to reach you.

A virtual entrance may be useful wherever prospective customers, families, students or visitors need to speak with someone at a physical location.

Example pilot setting

Schools & colleges

Parents and prospective students could connect with admissions or reception staff without first travelling to campus.

Clinics & hospitals

People could make general enquiries and reach the appropriate front-desk team remotely.

Hotels & hospitality

Prospective guests could speak with staff before deciding to visit or book.

Showrooms & offices

Customers could approach a real team member for product, service or appointment enquiries.

These are potential application areas. The best initial market will be selected through customer conversations and pilot feedback.
